The 7 Principles

Principle 7: The Right Size and Amount of Space for Use and Access

The right amount of size and room is given for users of all body types, postures, and movement to approach, reach, manipulate, and use.

Guidelines for Principle 7

Principle 7 is based on the following rules:

  • 7a. Make sure that users, whether they are sitting or standing, can see important parts clearly.
  • 7b. Make sure that all parts are easy for people sitting or standing to reach.
  • 7c. Allow for different-sized hands and grips.
  • 7d. Make sure there is enough room for people to use support gadgets or get special help.
    • (Centre for Excellence in Universal Design, 2025)
