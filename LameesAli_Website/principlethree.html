Principle 3: Use That Is Easy and Makes Sense

The style is easy to understand and use, no matter how much experience, information, language skills, or focus the person has at the moment.

Guidelines for Principle 3

Principle 3 is based on the following rules:

  • 3a. Get rid of any extraneous complications.
  • 3b. Be in line with what the person expects and what they feel.
  • 3c. Allow people with a wide range of reading and writing abilities to use it.
  • 3d. Put information in order based on how important it is.
  • 3e. Give good comments and reminders during and after the job is done.
