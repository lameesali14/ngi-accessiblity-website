Principle 6: Low Physical Effort

The design can be used effectively, comfortably, and with little effort.

Guidelines for Principle 6

Underlying Principle 6 are the following rules:

  • 6a. Let the user keep their body in a neutral position.
  • 6b. Adopt reasonable working forces.
  • 6c. Eliminate doing the same things over and over.
  • 6d. Refrain from too much strenuous physical work.
    • (Centre for Excellence in Universal Design, 2025)
