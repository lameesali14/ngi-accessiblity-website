Improvements

The National Gallery of Ireland is really thriving in trying to make it a very accessible location, which I really appreciate. But from my perspective — seeing things from the point of view of a wheelchair user — I would personally suggest trying to make the doors more accessible.

Wheelchair users should not have to rely on others to assist them all the time. From my experience, I did not want to depend on security guards or anyone else to open doors for me. Not all the doors in the gallery are accessible — only some. For me, that is the main area where I believe the National Gallery of Ireland could improve.

Conclusion

Overall, this experience was a real eye-opener. It placed me in a situation where I had to stop myself from using my legs — and that was not easy. It was so difficult to restrain myself. For example, when I wanted to open a door, I knew I could easily do it, and I would instinctively get up to open it. But then I would remind myself: no, I need to stay in the role, putting myself in someone else's shoes.

This exercise really showed me how challenging life can be for others. It gave me a completely different perspective on the world. Now, whenever I go somewhere, I automatically ask myself: Is this place accessible? If not, why not? What could be changed to make it accessible? This experience truly changed the way I see the world.
