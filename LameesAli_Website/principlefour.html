Principle 4: Information That Can Be Seen

The design makes sure that the person gets the information they need, no matter what the environment is like or how well they can sense things.

Guidelines for Principle 4

Principle 4 is based on the following rules:

  • 4a. Present important information in more than one way, such as through pictures, words, and touch.
  • 4b. Make sure there is enough difference between important information and what is around it.
  • 4c. Make the most important facts "legible."
  • 4d. Set things apart in ways that can be explained (i.e., make it simple to give orders or directions).
  • 4e. Make sure it works with a range of methods or tools that people who have sensory issues use.
    • (Centre for Excellence in Universal Design, 2025)
