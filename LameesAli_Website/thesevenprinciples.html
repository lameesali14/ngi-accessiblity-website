Seven Principles of Universal Design

Where did everything start?

The Seven Principles of Universal Design were created in 1997 by the late Ronald Mace and a group of architects, product designers, engineers, and environmental design researchers at North Carolina State University (NCSU).

These principles help guide the design of spaces, products, and communication to be more inclusive and accessible for everyone.

According to the Centre for Universal Design at NCSU, the principles "may be used to evaluate existing designs, guide the design process, and teach both designers and consumers about the features of more usable environments and products." (Centre for Excellence in Universal Design, 2025)
