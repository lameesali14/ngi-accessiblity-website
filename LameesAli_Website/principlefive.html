Principle 5: Allowing Mistakes to Happen

The design reduces risks and the bad effects of actions that are done by mistake or without meaning to.

Guidelines for Principle 5

Principle 5 is based on the following rules:

  • 5a. Arrange things so that mistakes and dangers are kept to a minimum: put the most-used things where they are easiest to reach; get rid of, separate, or hide dangerous things.
  • 5b. Let people know about risks and mistakes.
  • 5c. Make sure there are failsafes.
  • 5d. Do not let people do things without thinking when they need to be alert.
    • (Centre for Excellence in Universal Design, 2025)
