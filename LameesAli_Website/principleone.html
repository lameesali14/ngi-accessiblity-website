Principle 1: Use That Is Fair

The plan works for a wide range of skills and can be used and sold fairly to everyone.

Guidelines for Principle 1

Principle 1 is based on the following rules:

  • 1a. Give everyone the same way to use it: make it the same when you can, and make it similar when you cannot.
  • 1b. Do not put people in groups or give them bad names.
  • 1c. All users should have the same access to privacy, security, and safety features.
  • 1d. Make sure that everyone likes the style.
    • (Centre for Excellence in Universal Design, 2025)
