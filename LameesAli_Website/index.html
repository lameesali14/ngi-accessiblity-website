Wheelchair Accessibility in The National Art Gallery

This project is about walking in the shoes of others making one aware and to consider that all types of people are different and need certain necessities to interact with the physical and digital world. Universal design means that not one size fits all.

For this assignment we are to choose a venue/city/town/event or any place and to create an accessible website about the chosen place.
