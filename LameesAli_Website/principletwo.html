Principle 2: Use That Is Flexible

People with a wide range of tastes and skills can use the design. This principle ensures that the design accommodates a variety of individual preferences and abilities.

Guidelines for Principle 2

Principle 2 is based on the following rules:

  • 2a. Give people a choice of how to use it.
  • 2b. Allow entry and use by people who are right- or left-handed.
  • 2c. Make it easier for the user to be accurate and precise.
  • 2d. Allow the user to set their own pace.
